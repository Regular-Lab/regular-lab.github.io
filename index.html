We are a Europe-based AI R&D lab focused on exploring new ideas and approaches to machine intelligence.

We believe that the path forward toward greater intelligence is not just scaling and refining existing methods — it needs novel ideas and conceptual breakthroughs. Ideas that work across a broad range of tasks, that are robust to noise and real-world data, and that learn as they go.

Currently, we’re focused on reinforcement learning algorithms and world model approaches, applied across a range of domains — starting with challenging, unsolved games and simulated environments, and working our way toward real-world tasks.

You can learn more about us and how we think on our blog post. If our vision resonates with you, consider joining us.
